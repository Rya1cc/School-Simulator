What the inputs mean

Eventual admission: your estimated chance of ever receiving an offer. Waitlist chance: conditional chance of waitlisting if not admitted directly. WL → admit: chance of receiving an offer once waitlisted. Deferral: chance that a non-admitted, non-waitlisted early application continues to later review. Successful early applications may also be deferred in this illustrative model.

The model preserves each entered eventual admission probability. If waitlist assumptions imply more offers than that probability allows, the effective waitlist chance is reduced. Rolling applications have no modeled waitlist or deferral.

How rounds & stages work

EA / EA II / REA / ED: early notification, with possible later review. ED II: a later binding round, pending at the early stage. RD: regular review. Rolling: treated as available at the early stage for convenience, with no calendar dates. Final outcomes resolve all deferrals and waitlists.

ED and ED II are binding plans in practice. This tool compares hypothetical application outcomes and does not enforce withdrawals, deadlines, or restrictive early-action policies. Shared correlation controls a Gaussian latent factor, not the correlation between observed admit/deny decisions. No round receives an automatic probability boost. Waitlists-only mode simplifies timing and can show a waitlist as an early first decision.
